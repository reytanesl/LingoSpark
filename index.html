About LingoSpark

ESL vocab building and confidence boosting website.

LingoSpark is a growing collection of interactive ESL tools designed to make learning English fun, fast, and engaging. I build small, practical apps for teachers and learners—vocab games, writing challenges, team activities, and creative practice tools that turn everyday lessons into something students actually enjoy.

What I am really proud of is the Writing Suite, the feature that allows students to practice their writing skills and expand their glossaries individually and at their own pace. It can also be used by teachers as support for their writing classes.

More new activities coming soon!

If you like the project, want to test the AI features or support further development, your coffee helps me keep improving the platform, implementing new ideas and making English learning more appealing, natural and meaningful for everyone.

How to play — short guide

Open a section from the home squares, pick a game, then follow the setup screen if it asks for a word list.

Vocab Review

Classic Flashcards

Paste a glossary, flip each card, then mark Know / Don’t know. Unknown cards stay in the deck until you master them.

Vocabulary Bomb Defusal

Race the timer: type the matching word. Correct answers add time; mistakes take time away. Don’t let the bomb explode!

Grid Territory

Tic-tac-toe with vocab. Claim a square by answering correctly. Play solo vs a bot (bot skips questions) or two players/teams.

Vocab Auction

Bet chips on the word that matches the definition. Higher confidence can win more — wrong bids cost chips. Use built-in levels or your own list. Play solo or as 2 players taking alternating turns, each with their own bankroll. When you are logged in, every word and definition you play is added to your “Vocab Auction” set.

Team Challenge

Sentence Scramble

Race to rebuild correct sentences from scrambled words. Great on a classroom projector.

Quintagrams

Solve five clues using every jumbled letter exactly once.

Wordle

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries. Use letter colour feedback to narrow it down.

Word Chain

Connect vocabulary into chains under time pressure — keep the links valid and keep going.

Word Board

Build high-scoring words from letter tiles on a shared board with your team.

Writing Suite

Primary English

Detail Detective

Expand a short statement into a clearer, more detailed sentence with evidence.

Link Master

Join two sentences with because or so — keep the logic correct.

Exam Simulator

Write an exam-style email within the word limit, then get AI examiner feedback.

Dictation

Choose Beginner, A1 or A2 level. Listen & repeat aloud, or fill gaps — spelling must match exactly; capital letters do not matter.

AI games use A2 or B1 under Primary English. Beginner and A1 listening/spelling is in Dictation.

Advanced English

Frankenstein Builder

Drag sentence blocks into the right essay order (hook → context → thesis → evidence → analysis).

Transition Matcher

Choose the best discourse marker to join two academic statements smoothly.

The Devil’s Advocate

Read a thesis, then pick the strongest structural counter-argument from the options.

Bloat Slasher

Tap fluffy / wordy bits to cut them without losing meaning. Aim for a sharper sentence.

Vocabulary Upgrade

Replace everyday words with more precise academic alternatives.

Word-Forged Odyssey

Paste vocab, pick a genre, then write story actions that use the highlighted target words each turn.

Mission: Chat

Roleplay to achieve a goal. After the chat, get strengths and improvement feedback and export a PDF report.

Matura Writing Assessment

Upload a photo or PDF of a handwritten/printed essay plus the writing task. AI marks it against Matura rozszerzona writing criteria with detailed comments.
