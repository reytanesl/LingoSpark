About LingoSpark

ESL vocab building and confidence boosting website.

LingoSpark is a growing collection of interactive ESL tools designed to make learning English fun, fast, and engaging. I build small, practical apps for teachers and learners—vocab games, writing challenges, team activities, and creative practice tools that turn everyday lessons into something students actually enjoy.

What I am really proud of is the Writing Suite, the feature that allows students to practice their writing skills and expand their glossaries individually and at their own pace. It can also be used by teachers as support for their writing classes.

How to play — short guide

Open a section from the home squares, pick a game, then follow the setup screen if it asks for a word list.

Vocab Review

Classic Flashcards

Paste a glossary, flip each card, then mark Know / Don’t know. Unknown cards stay in the deck until you master them.

Test Countdown

Set your vocab test date, pick a daily budget, and follow a plan of new words plus reviews that peaks on the day — Know/Don’t know, typed recall, or mixed.

Memory Flip

Find matching term-definition pairs by flipping two cards at a time. Clear the board in as few attempts as possible.

Word Repair

You get a definition plus a broken spelling. Type the correct term to repair it and build a streak.

Speed Match Sprint

Race the clock: tap the term that matches each definition. Correct answers add time and streak points; mistakes cost seconds.

Vocabulary Bomb Defusal

Race the timer: type the matching word. Correct answers add time; mistakes take time away. Don’t let the bomb explode!

Grid Territory

Tic-tac-toe with vocab. Claim a square by answering correctly. Play solo vs a bot (bot skips questions) or two players/teams.

Vocab Auction

Bet chips on the word or phrase that matches the definition. Higher confidence can win more — wrong bids cost chips. Use a built-in level or your own list. Play solo or as 2 players. When you are logged in, every term and definition you play is added to your “Vocab Auction” set.

Penalty Match

A word and a meaning flash up together. Tap MATCH (or press Space) when they are a real pair: +10. Tapping a wrong pair costs 5, letting a real pair slip by costs 2, and letting a wrong pair pass is the right call. First to 100 wins. Pick a built-in level, a demo set, one of your word sets or the list you loaded in LingoSpark; choose Slow, Medium or Fast.

Share or export a word list

In Word Sets, use Share to copy a student link, or Export to download a clean glossary (Term = Definition, one per line). Students open a share link without signing in — the glossary loads into Vocab Review. Use the unlink button to turn sharing off.

Dictation

Choose Beginner, A1 or A2 level. Listen & repeat aloud, or fill gaps — spelling must match exactly; capital letters do not matter.

Colour Blocks

Build sentences with colour tiles (one word or phrase each: a / an, have to, don’t + have to). Creator mode also has to be (am / is / are), and / or, question words, and . or ?. Ages 10–12 add play/do/go collocations, daily routines, parts of the day, and telling the time. Fill the gaps: drag tiles into blanks. When you choose singular or plural, the plural form is shown and spoken. Persons: I, You, We, They, He, She. Things are grouped (food, furniture, school…). Line Up: drag pictured tiles onto numbered boxes. Switch EN | PL for help. Signed-in players collect each correct sentence in a Colour Blocks set.

Vocab Scenes

Interactive classroom scenes — tap objects to see and hear words. Labels, Sound and Quiz toggles in the toolbar. Opens in a new tab — free to play.

Topic Challenge

Premium practice on class topics with real scene pictures. Picture mode is Spot & Say (circle → question about that thing). Also switch Tiles / Transform when they fit the selected grammar. Optional speaking (Hear model + mic or “I said it”). Near-miss picture answers can be re-checked by AI; Ask why explains after a wrong attempt.

Review Chatbot

Premium teaching chatbot (text and/or speech). Set class, ease, grammar and vocab. Choose English chat or Polish tutor (coach quizzes in Polish; student answers in English). Optional conversation focus and Topic Challenge picture. End session for a coach note.

AI games use A2 or B1 under Primary English. Beginner and A1 listening/spelling is in Dictation.

Conciseness strategies
  • Eliminate redundancy (e.g. future plans → plans)
  • Use strong verbs (e.g. conduct an investigation → investigate)
  • Prefer active voice
  • Cut fillers (very, really, in order to, the fact that)
  • Shorten phrases (at this point in time → now)
  • Avoid nominalizations (turn noun phrases back into verbs)
  • Simplify long, complex sentence structure
