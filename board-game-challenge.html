Rules of Engagement

1. Sequential Roll & Hopping Movement: Scholars roll the die on their turn. The scholar token visibly hops across the board tile-by-tile to its designated landing space.
2. The Question Trial: Landing on any space triggers a random task from the active curriculum. If verified Correct, the scholar holds their ground. If Incorrect, the scholar is penalized and moves back.
3. Trap & Bonus Spaces: Bonus Spaces award an extra +2 leap forward! Trap Spaces enforce a severe -2 retreat on an incorrect answer.
4. The Exact Roll Requirement: To reach the Finish square, a scholar must roll the exact number of spaces needed. If a higher number is rolled, the scholar remains on their square and the turn passes immediately.
5. The Grand Thesis: Landing exactly on the Finish triggers the master final question. Answer correctly to claim victory.
